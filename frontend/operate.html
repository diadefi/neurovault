1Connect your wallet (owner)

Switches MetaMask to the configured network, adding it if needed. This address becomes the vault's immutable owner.

2Fund the burner (keeper + deployer)

The burner key is generated and kept in this browser. It deploys the demo contracts and calls poke(). It cannot withdraw or change owner settings. As mock-oracle admin, it can change prices and trigger a stop. Its private key is stored in this browser; use test funds only.

3Deploy the contracts (burner signs)

MockUSDC, MockOracle (settable demo feed), MockPerp (venue) and MockPerpNeuroVault with your address as owner. Mints venue liquidity and 1,000 mUSDC of collateral into the vault.

4Authorize setup (your wallet)

Two owner transactions: sign the spiking-neuron parameters, then open a $5,000 long with 1,000 mUSDC collateral. Subsequent pokes need no owner signature. Owner actions still require your wallet.

This alternate runner time-compresses leak and memory ×12 (τ = 50 s, memory 150 s), targeting ~5 s between pokes with a 4 s minimum. Receipts add wall-clock delay. Thresholds and trails are unchanged. The existing published vault uses normal timing (τ = 600 s, memory 1,800 s, cooldown 60 s).

5Run the market (burner pokes)

The burner moves the demo oracle along a seeded price path (calm, then a volatility burst at step 40) and calls the permissionless poke() after each move.
